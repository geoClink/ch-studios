Privacy Policy

KnotEmployee · CH Studios

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Overview

KnotEmployee is a scheduling and workforce management app built for small businesses. Managers use it to build schedules and track hours; employees use it to clock in and out, request time off, swap shifts, and message their team. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it.

Information We Collect

KnotEmployee collects only what it needs to run the app:

  • Account information — your name, email address, job title, and hourly rate. This is used to identify you within your workplace and is visible only to you and your manager.
  • Clock events — the timestamps when you clock in, start a break, end a break, and clock out. These are used to calculate hours worked.
  • Schedule data — your assigned shifts and your weekly availability preferences.
  • Time off requests — the dates you request, the type of time off (PTO, sick, or personal), and any optional notes you choose to include.
  • Shift swap requests — the shifts involved and any optional notes you add when requesting or accepting a swap.
  • Messages — direct messages and broadcast messages sent within the app. These are stored so your team can read and respond to them.
  • Push notification token — a device token provided by Apple that lets us deliver notifications to your device. It is stored securely and never shared with third parties.
  • Notification history — a log of in-app notifications so you can review them after they arrive.

What We Do Not Collect

KnotEmployee does not access or collect any of the following:

  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or camera
  • Health or biometric data
  • Browsing history

We also do not run any advertising or sell data to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used for one purpose: making the app work for you and your team.

  • Account information lets managers identify employees and assign them to shifts.
  • Clock events are used to calculate hours worked for payroll and reporting.
  • Schedule data powers the schedule builder and lets employees see their upcoming shifts.
  • Time off and shift swap requests flow through an approval process between employees and managers.
  • Messages allow your team to communicate within the app without needing a separate platform.
  • Your push notification token is used solely to deliver notifications — shift reminders, schedule updates, and message alerts.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

Third-Party Services

KnotEmployee uses two third-party services to function:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account data, clock events, schedules, messages, and requests are stored in Supabase. You can review their data practices at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) — used to deliver notifications to your device. Apple receives your device token as part of this process. APNs is operated by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or other third-party data services.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication and storage are handled by Supabase, which uses industry-standard security practices. Push notification tokens are stored securely and never exposed in logs or shared outside the app's backend.

No security system is perfect. If you have concerns about your account, contact us using the email below.

Your Rights

You have control over your data:

  • Access — you can view your account information, clock history, schedule, and messages directly in the app.
  • Correction — contact your manager or reach us directly to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — you can request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Portability — you can request a copy of your data by contacting us.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Clock and schedule records may be kept for up to one year after your last active shift to support payroll recordkeeping. If you request deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
