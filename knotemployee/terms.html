Description of Service

KnotEmployee is a scheduling and workforce management app built for small businesses. It is developed and operated by CH Studios Detroit. The app provides the following functionality:

  • For employees — clock in and out, view assigned schedules, request time off, propose shift swaps, and message coworkers.
  • For managers — build and publish schedules, approve or deny time off and shift swap requests, track labor costs, and broadcast messages to the team.

KnotEmployee is a tool for workplace coordination. It is not a payroll processor and does not issue wages or tax documents.
