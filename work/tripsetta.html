Overview

Tripsetta is a travel platform built for two audiences: solo travelers who want to plan and book trips in one place, and travel agents who manage clients, itineraries, and revenue through a dedicated Agent Mode. The app handles trip creation, bookings, task tracking, live travel news, messaging, and Tripi — an AI travel copilot that helps users plan itineraries, pack lists, and destinations on demand.

For agents, Tripsetta doubles as a business tool: a built-in CRM tracks leads and clients, a reporting dashboard shows revenue, and a client portal lets travelers access their own trip details — all inside the same app.

What It Does

Trip Planning

Create trips, attach bookings, build itineraries, and manage tasks — all in one view.

AI Copilot — Tripi

Ask for destination ideas, packing lists, or full itineraries. Responds instantly inside the app.

Explore & Discover

Travel tools, live news, currency converter, language translate, and destination inspiration in one view.

Agent Mode

A separate dashboard for travel agents with revenue overview, active trips, CRM, tasks, and messages.

CRM for Agents

Track leads, active clients, and follow-ups. Filter by status and manage the full sales pipeline.

Messaging

Built-in messaging keeps client conversations and group trip chats organized alongside the itinerary.
