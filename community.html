Built in Detroit.
For Detroit.

CH Studios was built by two Detroit-based developers who got their start through a community program — the Apple Developer Academy. We haven't forgotten that. Each quarter we reserve time to build technology for Detroit nonprofits, community organizations, and local causes that are doing meaningful work but don't have the resources for a software team.

Our Commitment

This isn't leftover time. We reserve dedicated hours each quarter specifically for community projects — the same quality and care we bring to every client, for organizations that are building up Detroit.

Native iPhone apps built with SwiftUI — volunteer management, event registration, community directories, donor portals, or whatever your organization actually needs.

Websites

Fast, accessible, mobile-first websites that tell your story, accept donations, and show up on Google. No templates. Built the right way.

Web Apps & Dashboards

If you're managing volunteers, tracking programs, or coordinating events across a team — we build the tools that replace the spreadsheets.
